Elara Etelier by H.K. — bridal lehengas and wedding dresses, made by hand in Delhi

Elara Etelier by H.K. — also written Elara Atelier, Elara HK or Elara by H.K. — is the couture label of designer Harneet Kaur, based in New Delhi and found online at elarabyhk.com. Harneet Kaur designs every piece herself. If you were looking for Harneet Kaur’s couture, bridal or festive work, you are in the right place.

Elara Etelier by H.K. is a couture etelier in New Delhi. Harneet Kaur and a small team of karigars cut, drape and embroider every garment by hand — bridal lehengas, wedding dresses, festive ethnic wear and contemporary Indian silhouettes — made to the measurements of the person who will wear them. Brides come to us from across Delhi NCR, including Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.

What is made here

  • Bridal lehengas with hand zardozi, dabka and sequin work
  • Wedding and reception gowns in Indian and fusion cuts
  • Sangeet, mehndi and haldi outfits in lighter weights
  • Festive kurta sets, shararas and anarkalis
  • Trousseau pieces and outfits for the wedding party

See the collections or commission something bespoke.

How long it takes

A bridal commission runs ten to fourteen weeks from first sketch to final fitting, depending on how dense the embroidery is. Festive and occasion pieces take two to four weeks. Brides marrying in the winter season should begin by late summer so there is room for three fittings without hurry.

Appointments

The etelier receives by appointment only, six days a week. First consultations can happen over WhatsApp before anyone travels — send the date, the occasion and a sense of what you have in mind, and we will take it from there. Enquire here.

Questions we are asked most

Can a piece from a collection be changed?
Yes. Fabric, colour, embroidery density, neckline and length are all open on most pieces, and several are made only to order. What is shown is a starting point, not a fixed garment.

How many fittings are there?
Three, as a rule: a measurement sitting, a muslin toile once the pattern is cut, and a final fitting close to the date. Brides outside Delhi can send measurements and have the toile posted to them.

What does a bridal commission cost?
It depends on the fabric, the hours of hand work and the scale of the ensemble, so each one is quoted on its own. Prices shown against pieces are a guide.

Do you work with brides outside Delhi?
Regularly — across Gurugram and Noida, and further afield by post once measurements are settled. About Harneet Kaur · inside the etelier.
